Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Sully is a private app used only by its owner to read and organize his own Gmail and Google Calendar. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own Google accounts, and only after he signs in and grants permission.

How data is used

Data is used only to help the owner manage his own email and calendar. It is processed on his own computer. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
